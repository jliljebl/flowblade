Editing Clips on Timeline

Trimming Clips

Use Multitrim Tool.

Moving Clips

Use Insert Tool, Move Tool or Spacer Tool.

Cutting Clips

  1. Select cut frame.
    • Cut is always made before the currently displayed frame on all active tracks.
  2. Cut clip.
    • Press Cut button or press X on the keyboard.

Deleting Clips

Splice Out

Select clip(s) and press the Delete key or use the Splice Out button on the middle bar.

  • The selected clip(s) are deleted.
  • All clips after the deleted clip(s) on the track are moved backward to cover the empty space.

Lift

Select clip(s) and press the Lift button on the middle bar.

  • The selected clip(s) are replaced with blanks.
  • Other clips on the track are not moved.
  • If the selected clip is in the middle of the track, a blank is left in its place.

Ripple Delete

Select clip(s) and press the Ripple Delete button on the middle bar.

  • The selected clip(s) are deleted.
  • Clips on all tracks after the deleted clip(s) are moved backward to cover the empty space.
  • If moving the clips backward would cause an overwrite on the timeline, the edit is not allowed.

Range Delete

Set Mark In and Mark Out on the timeline and press the Range Delete button on the middle bar.

  • All clips and blanks on all tracks within the marked range are deleted.
  • Everything after the deleted range is moved backward by the length of the deleted range.

Fade/Transition Cover Delete

Select a single rendered fade or transition clip and press the Delete key or button.

  • If the clips before and after the deleted clip have frames available, the empty space is covered using material from the adjacent clips.
  • If sufficient material is not available, a splice out is performed.
  • A preference is available to always perform a splice out when deleting fades and transitions.

Use Keyboard Shortcuts!

It is much faster to use keyboard shortcuts than to use the buttons for the same operations:

  • Press X to cut clips.
  • Press Delete to splice out clips.
